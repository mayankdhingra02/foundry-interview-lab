# Learner-visible retention rules

Settings includes a server-rendered storage notice based on the actual `RETENTION_POLICY` constant, rather than an independently hard-coded promise. Rendering reads no learner store, financial ledger or provider account. The notice is available even when account/provider capabilities are unconfigured.

It distinguishes canonical attempts retained until learner deletion, no application audio-replay persistence, operational metadata eligible after 30 days, settled usage groups eligible after 62 days, protected active/pending/uncertain obligations and separate support/payment records. Those are earliest maintenance eligibility periods, not maximum storage durations or evidence that a schedule exists. The current bounded operator maintenance remains manual.

Downloaded exports cannot be revoked. Backups, providers and approved monitoring have independent policies; deleting in the app does not establish instant upstream erasure. Hosted approval of the full policy, actual scheduler setup, backup expiration and provider contractual verification remain release requirements. The notice does not invent an approved deployment-specific policy URL or claim a running cleanup schedule.

The narrow browser check intercepts account/capability calls, invokes no mutation and verifies the periods, exceptions and 390px reading layout. It does not qualify upstream deletion or legal/commercial obligations. See [retention.md](retention.md) for the exact maintenance implementation and operator procedure.
